## 2026-09-26 16:10 No page cap: whole textbooks in, ticked concepts out
- **Context:** The user wants a full PDF (not a <= 40-page chapter) uploaded, broken into concepts they pick from, and only the picked concepts fed to the game-making agents.
- **Decisions:** (1) `POST /api/sources` drops the 40-page cap; the byte (200 MB) and text (2M chars) limits are memory guards only. (2) The Gatekeeper reads a digest of long documents (first 3 pages complete, later pages by their opening ~320 chars, 120k-char budget) so it can still outline a whole book. (3) The Curriculum agent reads long documents in section-aligned parts cut at the gatekeeper outline (<= 40 pages / 40k chars per part, sections under 25k chars folded into a neighbor; retuned from 12 pages / 4k after the first live run on a real calculus textbook turned a 36-page excerpt into 6 parts and 27 slivers of units, and the part prompt now asks for 1-3 units per part), in parallel under runAgent cap of 6, and `mergeCurriculumSlices` keeps ids unique across parts; short material is a single unchanged call, so the sample fixtures and prompts are byte-identical. Mock mode never chunks (canned replies are per sample). (4) The intake page lists every concept with a checkbox (all ticked up to 25 concepts, none above); `Intake.conceptIds` carries the selection and `startGameJob` runs on `selectConcepts(km, ids)`, so regenerate keeps it (`intakeFromSpec` reads it back from `spec.concepts`). (5) The prep pre-check covers the whole map, so a ticked subset gets its own three questions from `POST /api/sources/:id/precheck`, cached per selection key and looked up again by `POST /api/games` (never trusting the client items); when the Pre-check Writer fails (in mock mode, for any non-sample selection) `derivePreCheck` builds them from misconceptions and facts. (6) The mock Director now reads the job concept ids from the prompt `# Concepts` block, so a subset selection works end to end in mock mode.
